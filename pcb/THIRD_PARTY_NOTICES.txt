This is a generated board design asset, not a redistributed component-library collection.
The custom F1 land pattern has no fitted fuse body model. R14/R15 are DNP and
excluded from the GLB; all pads remain in the actual board geometry and 2D inspector.

The iBOM inspector covers front/back outer copper. Its two empty outer-zone arrays
are expected: this board's two filled zones are on internal copper layers.
The native GLB export includes inner copper, and native layer PDFs remain authoritative.
No native application editing or hardware qualification is provided in the browser.
