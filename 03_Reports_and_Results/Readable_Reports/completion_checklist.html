The executed validation report records DIGITAL SCOPE COMPLETE: the numerical/CAD tests passed with no failures, errors or skips, native schematic ERC/PCB DRC/connectivity passed, and the versioned wheel passed detached installation checks. The public repository and its versioned releases provide publication evidence, checked separately from local engineering acceptance. The current published release is linked below.
