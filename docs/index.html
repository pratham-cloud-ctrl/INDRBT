Read claims with their evidence class

TARGET is desired performance; ASSUMED is a model input; CALCULATED and SIMULATED are model outputs; VERIFIED DIGITALLY identifies an executed check. The 4 kg payload and ±0.1 mm repeatability remain targets. Sampled clearance, simplified PID and conceptual electronics do not establish physical qualification.
